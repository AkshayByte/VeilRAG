## 3. Analysis of the 16 False Positives (Presidio Built-ins & Phone Recognizer)

All 16 False Positives arose from existing detectors evaluating the intentionally planted hard negatives:

### Group A: Phone Numbers in Hard Negative Sentences (10 FPs)
The hard negative suite included corporate helplines, dispatch numbers, and switchboard contacts to verify that `BankAccountRecognizer` would not mistake 10-digit phone numbers for bank accounts.
While `BankAccountRecognizer` correctly ignored them, existing phone detectors flagged them:
- **`INDIAN_MOBILE` (8 FPs)**:
  - `"For technical questions, please dial customer helpdesk 8844220011."` -> `8844220011`
  - `"Dispatch coordinator emergency contact line is 7733119900."` -> `7733119900`
  - `"Direct office telephone line for facilities is 9988112233."` -> `9988112233`
  - `"Enterprise vendor support desk answers calls at 8822446600."` -> `8822446600`
  - `"Central reception switchboard telephone number is 6644220088."` -> `6644220088`
- **`PHONE_NUMBER` (2 FPs)**:
  - Presidio's built-in phone recognizer also triggered on the same corporate phone numbers.

### Group B: Presidio Built-in US SSN on Order Tracking Codes (5 FPs)
Presidio's built-in `UsSsnRecognizer` flagged 9-digit numeric sequences embedded within logistics tracking numbers and barcode manifests:
- `"Warehouse shipment requisition #9988776655 has cleared export customs."` -> `998877665` flagged as `US_SSN`.
- `"Cargo tracking identifier 223344556677 indicates delivery to airport hub."` -> `223344556` flagged as `US_SSN`.
- `"Procurement dispatch note PO-8877665544 signed by receiving officer."` -> `887766554` flagged as `US_SSN`.
- `"Air waybill tracking number 334455667788 shows transit through Doha."` -> `334455667` flagged as `US_SSN`.
- `"Container freight manifest 112233445566 inspected at dry port."` -> `112233445` flagged as `US_SSN`.

### Group C: SpaCy Statistical NER False Positive (1 FP)
- **`PERSON` (1 FP)**:
  - spaCy's `en_core_web_lg` model flagged a capitalized technical term in clean prose as a person name.

The four coverage recognizers implemented for Prompt 4A performed with **100% precision and 100% recall** on the held-out dataset, demonstrating complete robustness against hard negative confounders.
