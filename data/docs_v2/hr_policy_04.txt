### 1. Objective and Operating Scope
This comprehensive employment handbook defines the service terms, professional obligations, code of workplace ethics, and operational standards applicable to all full-time, part-time, and probationary personnel employed by Quantum Solstice Labs Private Limited across all corporate campuses and delivery hubs. Adherence to this operational framework is a strict mandatory condition of employment.

### 2. Standard Working Hours and Core Attendance Guidelines
The official operating business hours of the enterprise are designated as 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM with core agile hours, comprising an eight-hour productive workday alongside an allotted one-hour window for lunch and personal refreshment. Associates assigned to operational rotations must observe assigned shifts under direct departmental oversight. Digital biometric timestamps are mandatory for campus ingress and egress.

### 3. Annual Leave Entitlements and Separation Notice Period
Each confirmed associate is granted 21 days of paid privilege leave per calendar cycle, accrued proportionately on a monthly basis. Associates are also entitled to twelve days of medical contingency leave subject to clinical certification. Upon voluntary resignation or formal separation, the mandatory contractual notice period is exactly 45 calendar days. During the initial statutory probation period of 3 months, separation notice may be executed within thirty days.
