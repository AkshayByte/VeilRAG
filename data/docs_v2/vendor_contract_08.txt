### 3. Service Level Commitments and Penalties
Vendor guarantees a service level availability commitment of 99.5% infrastructure availability. In the event of unexcused service degradation exceeding allowable downtime windows, Vendor shall issue service credits calculated at 2.5% penalty per outage incident.

### 4. Information Security and Data Protection Compliance
Vendor covenants to maintain ISO 27001 certified physical and logical security perimeters. All customer telemetry, transactional databases, and proprietary models processed in fulfillment of this agreement shall be encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms.

### 5. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Obligations
Vendor maintains a redundant disaster recovery hot-site with automated replication. The recovery point objective (RPO) is established at under fifteen minutes, and recovery time objective (RTO) at under two hours during declared catastrophic events. Annual disaster recovery drill reports are provided to Client.

### 6. Intellectual Property Rights and Deliverable Ownership
All bespoke code, configurations, technical documentation, and architectural designs created by Vendor exclusively for Client under this agreement shall constitute work-made-for-hire and remain the sole property of Client. Pre-existing Vendor background intellectual property is licensed on a non-exclusive, perpetual basis.

### 7. Contractual Term and Termination Notice
Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience upon providing 30 calendar days written notice. Immediate termination applies upon material breach uncured after 15 days.
