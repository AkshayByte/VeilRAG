### 1. Objective and Operating Scope

### 2. Standard Working Hours and Core Attendance Guidelines
The official operating business hours of the enterprise are designated as 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through Friday, comprising an eight-hour productive workday alongside an allotted one-hour window for lunch and personal refreshment. Associates assigned to operational rotations must observe assigned shifts under direct departmental oversight. Digital biometric timestamps are mandatory for campus ingress and egress.

### 3. Annual Leave Entitlements and Separation Notice Period
Each confirmed associate is granted 28 days of paid privilege leave per calendar cycle, accrued proportionately on a monthly basis. Associates are also entitled to twelve days of medical contingency leave subject to clinical certification. Upon voluntary resignation or formal separation, the mandatory contractual notice period is exactly 90 calendar days. During the initial statutory probation period of 6 months, separation notice may be executed within thirty days.
