# PINNACLE URBAN SOLUTIONS LIMITED
## COMMERCIAL INDENTURE OF LEASE AND PREMISES LICENSE
Indenture ID: LEASE-V2-LEASE_AGREEMENT_07
Term: 36 Months Fixed | Tenancy Commencement: 1st March 2026

### 1. Demised Premises and Parties to the Indenture
This Commercial Indenture of Lease is entered into between Pinnacle Urban Solutions Limited ('Lessor') represented by Property Custodian Bhavini Saxena and Lessee TerraTrust Capital ('Lessee') represented by Ubika Khosla. Lessor demises commercial office space for exclusive enterprise technology operations.

### 2. Rent Consideration, Security Deposit, and Periodic Escalation
Lessee covenants to pay Lessor a net monthly lease rental of INR 6,50,000 per month, payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. Prior to handover, Lessee deposits an interest-free refundable security deposit of INR 58,50,000 (9 months rent). The rental consideration shall be subject to 9% escalation every 3 years.

### 3. Tenancy Term and Mandatory Lock-In Period
The initial tenancy term is 36 months, subject to a 36 months commercial lock-in. Neither party may terminate the tenancy during the lock-in without full rental compensation.

### 4. Permitted Commercial Use and Facility Maintenance
The demised premises shall be utilized exclusively for lawful software engineering and administrative office activities. Lessee shall maintain interior fixtures, electrical distributions, and air conditioning equipment in good tenantable order. Structural repairs, exterior facades, and common building utilities remain the responsibility of Lessor.

### 5. Handover Protocol and Demised Premises Surrender
Upon expiry or valid termination of the lease indenture, Lessee covenants to peaceably deliver vacant possession of the demised premises to Lessor in good original condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted. All Lessee installed modular partitions, communication cables, and corporate signages shall be cleanly removed.

### 6. Premises Insurance and Mutual Indemnification
Lessee shall maintain comprehensive commercial general liability insurance and fire property insurance covering all internal assets and tenant improvements. Lessor maintains standard building structural cover. Each party indemnifies the other against direct third-party tort claims arising from gross operational negligence.
