# ACME SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED
## COMPREHENSIVE HUMAN RESOURCES EMPLOYMENT POLICY AND CODE OF CONDUCT
Document Reference: HR-POL-2026-BLR-01
Effective Date: 1st January 2026 | Revision: 4.2

### 1. Objective and Applicability
This comprehensive employment handbook defines the service terms, professional obligations, code of workplace ethics, and operational standards applicable to all full-time, part-time, and probationary personnel employed by Acme Software Solutions Private Limited across all its technology development centers in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune. Adherence to this framework is a mandatory condition of continuing employment.

### 2. Standard Working Hours and Attendance Guidelines
The official operating business hours of the corporation are firmly designated as 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM Monday through Friday, comprising an eight-hour productive workday alongside an allotted one-hour window for lunch and personal refreshment. Engineering associates assigned to client operational engagements may observe staggered rotas under direct managerial dispensation. All personnel are required to record biometric digital timestamps upon ingress and egress at company premises.

### 3. Annual Leave Entitlements and Separation Notice Period
Each confirmed associate is granted 24 days of paid privilege leave per calendar cycle, accrued proportionately on a monthly basis. Associates also receive twelve days of paid casual sickness allowances subject to clinical certification. Upon voluntary resignation or formal separation, the mandatory contractual notice period is exactly 60 calendar days. During the initial statutory probation period of 6 months, separation notice may be executed within thirty calendar days by either party.
