# COMMERCIAL EQUIPMENT ASSET FINANCE AND CREDIT FACILITY AGREEMENT
## FACILITY SANCTION ORDER: ICICI-EQUIP-2026-DEL-104
Dated: 24th January 2026 | New Delhi Commercial Branch

### 1. Commercial Loan Facility Purpose
This Secured Asset Finance Agreement is established between Apex Commercial Credit Corporation (the Financier) and Precision CNC Heavy Industries Private Limited (the Enterprise Borrower). The facility is granted solely for the procurement and installation of high-precision German 5-axis computer numerical control (CNC) milling machinery for automotive part fabrication.

### 2. Sanctioned Amount, Tenure, and Interest Rate Terms
The Financier sanctions an asset loan amount of INR 1,25,00,000 (Rupees One Crore Twenty-Five Lakhs Only) repayable over a tenure of 84 months. The applicable interest rate is 11.25% per annum fixed, calculated on monthly reducing balance and not subject to market interest index fluctuation. In the event the borrower exercises voluntary loan prepayment or foreclosure prior to maturity, a prepayment penalty of 2.0% of outstanding principal shall be levied plus applicable statutory taxes.

### 4. Hypothecation of Financed Machinery and Insurance Cover
The underlying CNC equipment shall remain exclusively hypothecated in favor of the Financier until issuance of No Objection Certificate (NOC). The Enterprise Borrower covenants to maintain comprehensive machinery breakdown and fire insurance policies at all times, with the Financier endorsed as sole loss payee.

### 5. Financial Covenants and Default Remedies
The Enterprise Borrower shall maintain a Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) of not less than 1.35 throughout the loan tenure. Default in interest payment exceeding 30 days shall attract default penal interest at 24% per annum compounded monthly.
