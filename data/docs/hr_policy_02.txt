# BHARAT LOGISTICS AND SUPPLY CHAIN ENTERPRISES
## OPERATIONAL WORKFORCE REGULATIONS AND SERVICE GOVERNANCE MANUAL
Manual Reference: BL-HR-REG-2026-MUM-02
Published: 15th January 2026 | Authority: Board of Directors

### 1. Scope and Preamble
Bharat Logistics operates warehousing nodes, intermodal freight corridors, and delivery depots across western and northern India. This operational directive governs all station supervisors, dispatch handlers, fleet coordinators, and administrative executives. Consistent workplace discipline and strict adherence to transport safety norms are strictly enforced across all distribution hubs.

### 2. Duty Hours, Shift Scheduling, and Field Attendance
Field operations, supply logistics, and hub management observe designated shift timings: standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Monday through Saturday. Hub supervisors managing 24/7 cross-dock operations are rostered on three rotating eight-hour cycles. Punctuality at depot gates is tracked electronically; unauthorized absence exceeding three consecutive shifts without intimation shall be deemed job abandonment.

### 3. Leave Policy and Separation Protocols
Permanent operations personnel receive 18 days of paid privilege leave annually, with up to nine days eligible for cash encashment upon financial year closure. Ten statutory festival holidays are notified annually per regional labour commissioner bulletins. In consideration of operational business continuity across freight channels, the mandatory notice period is 90 calendar days for all confirmed staff. The initial probation period is 3 months, during which performance evaluations occur fortnightly.

### 4. Workplace Welfare, Safety, and Grievance Escalation
All warehouse associates must wear personal protective equipment, safety boots, and high-visibility jackets inside loading bays. Handlers transporting volatile chemicals, lithium batteries, or temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals must maintain certified manifests. Negligent handling resulting in environmental contamination shall trigger immediate civil and criminal indemnification procedures.
