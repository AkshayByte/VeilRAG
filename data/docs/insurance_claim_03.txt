# ICICI LOMBARD GENERAL INSURANCE COMPANY
## COMMERCIAL PROPERTY AND ASSET LOSS CLAIM MEMORANDUM
Policy Docket: COMM-FIRE-2025-4421 | Docket Ref: LOMB-CLM-2026-KOL-03
Eastern Regional Office | Kolkata, West Bengal

### 1. Risk Location and Event Description
A fire broke out in the packaging storage wing of Eastern Garment Export Enterprises, Sector 5, Salt Lake, Kolkata. The official claim number generated in the insurer ERP is CLM-FIRE-2026-11045. The licensed surveyor assessment concluded: Electrical short-circuit without gross negligence in the secondary distribution box.

### 2. Loss Verification and Approved Quantum
The insured entity submitted itemized replacement estimates for damaged cardboard cartons, corrugated boxes, and packing machinery reflecting a claimed amount of INR 14,20,000. Following inspection of stock purchase registers and application of under-insurance factors, the surveyor recommended an approved amount of INR 12,80,000 in full and final settlement of all material damage claims.

### 4. Environmental and Municipal Approvals
The Kolkata Municipal Corporation Fire Services issued Fire Incident Certificate confirming deployment of 3 tender vehicles. No structural integrity damage was reported to the primary RCC building columns.

### 5. Risk Mitigation and Fire Safety Compliance Recommendations
The chartered loss adjuster recommends mandatory installation of automated smoke aspiration detectors in all storage warehouses. Electrical wiring in distribution bays must be enclosed within fire-retardant conduits and undergo annual thermal imaging audits. The insured partner undertakes to maintain fully charged CO2 and ABC dry chemical fire extinguishers at 15-meter intervals.
